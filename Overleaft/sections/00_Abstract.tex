\chapter*{Abstract}
\glsresetall

\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

\begin{spacing}{1.5}

Critical infrastructures increasingly depend on industrial supervision and control systems whose internal communication may be exposed when legacy protocols do not include cryptographic security. In the University of Cuenca Micro-Grid, this problem appears on the link between the \gls{SCADA} server and the \gls{API} station controller, where \gls{MODBUS}/\gls{TCP} traffic carries measurements, states, and commands without native mechanisms for confidentiality, integrity, or replay protection. In response, this work designs, implements, and validates a transparent secure channel compatible with the \gls{OT} environment, without modifying the industrial logic, controller addressing, or supervision process.


\end{spacing}

\vspace{0.4cm}

\hspace{1.27cm} \textit{Author Keywords:} industrial cybersecurity, SCADA, Modbus TCP, MACsec, transparent secure channel, Micro-Grid, OT.
